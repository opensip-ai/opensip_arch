**Classification rule for the rewrite** (column "Class"):
- `DESIGN-FINAL` — row lead label is `SATISFIED` and the design content is an independently accepted contract or an adopted D-000 decision. Carry into new docs as accepted design (with the named condition-4 remainder).

- `PROCESS-ONLY` — neither of the above: V1-prerequisite tracking, re-review gates, release qualification, deferrals, or a gate with no recorded occupancy. Archive only; carry deferral/scope decisions by reference.

**Status-token reading.** Per 08:L402–L404 the token is the *leading label* of the status cell; cells carry long prose after it. The "Live status" column quotes the leading label verbatim and marks where the cell continues.
