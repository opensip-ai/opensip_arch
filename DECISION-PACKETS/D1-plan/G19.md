**Risk.** The reviewer-attack pattern recorded on the two existing lineages maps onto G19 with
unusual force. (a) *Inventing a vocabulary the record does not carry* — g23 v1 M2/M3 rejected an
undeclared digest construction and an invented Coverage-domain encoding vocabulary; G19's highest
exposure is the same failure in EV-2's "store identity" and EV-5's purge-declaration bytes, where
there is no recorded schema for what a declaration record looks like, only that it must exist and be
in-class. That is the largest single risk on this gate (see **OQ-G19-2**). (b) *Collapsing two
classes the occupancy keeps separate* — g23 v2 M1 rejected a fixture that collapsed EV-2 into EV-3;
G19's analogue is collapsing EV-3 retention into EV-5 purge (both touch discard), or EV-4 backup into
EV-6 migration, both explicitly forbidden by `#$.liveCellMemberSplit.notCollapsed`. (c) *Classifying a
witness under a closed identifier it does not belong to* — g21 v3 M1 rejected an RF-2 case filed
under the closed CC-5 corpus; G19's analogue is filing a corruption or promotion witness under
`class-lifecycle-matrix` when `$.retainedEvidence[7].suppliedBy` assigns it to
`cross-class mutations`. (d) *Over-claiming closure* — g23 v1 S4 rejected an over-claiming
`leftoverDesignClosedIfAcceptedAndRecorded`; on G19 the same field must not claim DR-124 closure,
must not claim `OBL-GRANT-JOURNAL`/`OBL-INHERIT-BLOCKED`/`OBL-MONOTONIC` closure, and must not claim
GATE closure without the ROW successor (section 1.4).

## 7. Open questions

**OQ-G19-1:** Is the fixture-cell count 48 (32 matrix + 16 cross-class), or does each retained-evidence
member require its own separately digest-pinned record, giving 7 × 4 × 2 = 56 for EV-1..EV-7 plus
4 for EV-8? `harness…v2.json#$.namedCorpusClasses[0].exactByteIntent` says "Each cell retains the
seven first-group live-cell members for that class × operation", which reads as 32 cells each
carrying 7 members; but each of `$.retainedEvidence[0..6].exactByteIntent` says "per D-002 platform,
per included class, a digest-pinned … record" with no lifecycle-operation qualifier, which reads as
8 records per member independent of the operation axis. The record does not state which count the
corpus must carry. Not resolved here.

**OQ-G19-2:** What is the byte schema of a "digest-pinned declaration" record? Every
`$.retainedEvidence[*].exactByteIntent` requires "digest-pinned" bytes (declaration, retention,
backup/restore-scope, purge, migration, corruption, refusal records), and
`$.namedCorpusClasses[1].exactByteIntent` requires "digest-pinned refused writes", but no pinned
contract in `basedOn` defines a record schema, a serialization, or the digest construction —
`$.basedOn.stateClassContractV10.role` explicitly withholds v10's `bytePlacement`, and
`$.failsIf[18]` forbids applying v10/v11. g23-fixture-corpus.v1 was REJECTED on exactly this
(reviewer M2: the coverage commitment's SHA-256 construction was undeclared). Choosing a
serialization and digest construction is a witness choice only if it is declared in the corpus and
does not become a new encoding vocabulary; the record does not settle where that line falls for G19.

**OQ-G19-3:** How is the waiver rule `"all stateful first-slice roles"`
(`$.waiverRuleVerbatim`, file 08 line 355) discharged by a fixture corpus?
`$.passProperty` requires fixtures "for … every stateful first-slice role", and
`$.retainedEvidence[1].passProperty` says "Waiver of a stateful first-slice role is not a skipped
cell", but `$.statefulFirstSliceRoles.statefulSurfaces` says "This specification does not invent a
role catalog." No artifact in `basedOn` enumerates the stateful first-slice roles. The corpus
therefore cannot enumerate that axis without inventing it.

**OQ-G19-4:** Does the cross-class-mutations class require witnesses per *write direction* in
addition to the four named negative tests? `$.namedCorpusClasses[1].exactByteIntent` names both "refused
writes among the two included classes and from either included class toward Authoritative evidence"
(which reads as 2 intra-preview directions + 2 toward-Authoritative directions = 4 directions) and,
separately, "file 04's four named negative tests". `$.namedCorpusClasses[1].passProperty` names three
distinct fates plus a sealed-evidence fate. Section 2 counts the four named negative tests (16 cells);
whether the direction sub-axis multiplies that to 16 directions × 4 platforms is not settled by the
record.

**OQ-G19-5:** Is EV-1 (`Plan/Run consequence`) constructible at all in a preview corpus without a
Plan/Run identity encoding? `$.retainedEvidence[0].exactByteIntent` requires "a digest-pinned
Plan/Run-identity record showing (1) a cache hit equals recomputation and does not enter Plan/Run as a
new input, (2) operational-metadata fields are absent from Plan/Run identity", but
`$.previewClassAxis.cacheKeys` says "Cache/index keys and regeneration-key producing rules ride DR-006
and are not settled here" and `$.failsIf[37]` fails the specification if "DR-006
cache/regeneration-key recipes are treated as settled". Constructing a cache-hit-equals-recomputation
witness appears to need a regeneration key. The record does not say how to construct the witness
without one. This is the closest thing on G19 to a hard dependency; it is recorded as an open
question rather than as `BLOCKED-ON-DR-006` because no sentence in the record states that the fixture
cannot be completed without DR-006, and section 4 records the standing accordingly.

**OQ-G19-6:** Should the fixture corpus be a first-authoring (`fixtures/g19.v1/`, no platform
subdirectory, per `g21-fixture-corpus.v1`) or go straight to per-platform copies (per
`g23-fixture-corpus.v4`)? G19's occupancy carries `$.platforms`, and every passProperty ends "Pass all
four D-002 platforms", which argues for platform copies at v1; but the recorded first-authoring
precedent (`g21-fixture-corpus.v1`, D-241) puts the copy layer in a separate later version. The record
does not say which precedent governs a gate that has both a platforms array and no prior corpus.
