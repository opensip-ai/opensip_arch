component identity × 4 platforms. As a payload authoring in the `g21-fixture-corpus.v7` shape the
unit is the platform-free payload: **32 payloads**, followed by 128 copies at the copy version.

obligations in this plan set. No ROW twin, so each closure act is (corpus, `g21-leftover-join.vN`)

## 7. Open questions

**OQ-G21-1:** Is EV-4 (`sealed-evidence preservation`) one fixture or 32? Five of the six
retained-evidence members' `exactByteIntent` begin "Once authored: per class:", but
`harness…v4.json#$.retainedEvidence[3].exactByteIntent` reads "Once authored: a digest-pinned
sealed-evidence set present before injection; the same set digested after teardown. The fixture pins
both digests" — with no per-class qualifier. Whether the sealed-evidence set is authored once and
reused across all remaining classes, or once per class, is not stated.

**OQ-G21-2:** May several authored members share one `inputCorpusId`? The two authored CC-5 payloads
both cite `inputCorpusId` `"G21.cc.CC-5"`
(`g21-fixture-corpus.v7.json#$.authoredCatalog.members[0..1].inputCorpusId`), while the two NT
payloads cite distinct per-case ids. Ten more CC-5 injections would make eleven members sharing one
input id. The record does not say whether a `g21-input-corpus.v2` enumerating the twelve CC-5
injections as separate initial states must be authored first, nor whether the CC-1/CC-2/CC-4/CC-7
sub-axes (section 2's unenumerated axes) need their own input states.

**OQ-G21-3:** What negotiated `maxControlFrameBytes` does the `"CC-5 prefix one over the postHandshake
bound"` injection assume? `control-protocol-contract.v2.json#$.transportAndFraming.framing.bounds.postHandshake`
says "the operative bound is the negotiated maxControlFrameBytes (handshake field): the component may
accept less than the host offer but never less than 65536; the ceiling either side may offer or
accept is 16777216." Both 65536 and 16777216 are quotable constants, but a fixture must pick one
negotiated value, and `g21-fixture-corpus.v8.json#$.whatIsNotAuthored` lists "65536 or 16777216 as
newly invented bounds" and "26214400" among what is not authored. If the assumed bound is 65536, then
"one over" is 65537 — byte-identical to the already-authored
`G21.cc5.prefix-one-over-prehandshake` (sha256
`76cc5805dab9b4eacefdb477f498020fd82bccdbc9c6a2d9ce10586ac85512b4`), and the two injections would be
distinguishable only by session state, not by payload bytes. The record does not settle which value
to assume or how the distinction is pinned.

**OQ-G21-4:** How does a bare payload file express *postHandshake* state, a *truncated body*, or any
body-carrying CC-5 injection at all? Both authored CC-5 payloads are 4-byte prefix-only files
(`byteLength` `4`, `mutation`: "no body … because the receiver must refuse from the prefix alone").
Seven of the ten remaining injections (`truncated bodies`, `invalid UTF-8`, `duplicate members`,
`unknown members`, `floats`, `negative integers`, `over-uint53 integers`) require a body, and
`$.controlFrameEncoding.messageEnvelope` requires that body carry `type`, `seq`, `controlMajor`, and
`body` — but `g21-fixture-corpus.v8.json#$.whatIsNotAuthored` records "a ping, pong, hello, helloAck,
or other per-type body schema" as not authored. Whether a body may be constructed by quoting a `type`
token from `capabilityEnvelope.messageVocabulary` without authoring that type's body schema, and
whether a postHandshake fixture needs a session script rather than a payload file, is not settled.

**OQ-G21-5:** How many CC-1 orderings must the corpus carry?
`control-protocol-contract.v2.json#$.hostileDualChannelConformance.classes[0].intent` requires "Every
pairwise and every reachable total ordering of {cancel-issued, provider-final-octet, control-fault,
provider-fault-report, provider-EOF, process-death, teardown-initiated}" — 7 events, so 21 pairwise
orderings and up to 5040 total orderings, of which an unspecified subset is "reachable". The record
does not enumerate the reachable set, and D-293's default policy requires the quantifier be
preserved. The number is not supplied here.

**OQ-G21-6:** How many CC-2 split points? `#$.hostileDualChannelConformance.classes[1].intent`
requires "split at every byte offset of the provider frame's length prefix and at chunk sizes of 1
byte". The provider frame's length-prefix width is fixed by the provider contract
(`delivery.v2.json` per the wire pin), but the frame's total length is a fixture choice, and "chunk
sizes of 1 byte" over a whole frame is a per-octet quantifier over an unfixed length. Not enumerable
from the record.
