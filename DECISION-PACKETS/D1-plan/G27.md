**Risk.** The reviewer-attack pattern on the two recorded fixture-corpus lineages is: (a) inventing a
vocabulary or encoding the record does not carry, (b) collapsing two classes the occupancy keeps
separate, (c) classifying a witness under a closed identifier it does not belong to, (d) over-claiming
closure. **G27's dominant exposure is (a), in an unusually pure form.**

The whole corpus is a set of *absence* witnesses: no sealed-Run label, no later relabel, no G19
durable write. Making an absence legible pulls toward describing the thing that is absent, and the
moment a G27 payload carries a field like `sealed: false` or `runClass: "preview"` it has invented
the discriminator the record forbids. `g23-fixture-corpus.v1.review-independent.claude2.json`
returned `REJECT` with five MUST-FIX and four SHOULD-FIX, including
`M3 — hostRequestedDomainBaseline invents a Coverage-domain encoding vocabulary and under-determines
the requested domain`; the same reviewer would land the identical finding against a G27 payload that
sketched a sealed-Run shape in order to negate it. The occupancy anticipated this and wrote the
answer into EV-1's own `exactByteIntent`: `it retains the absence of that label`. A corpus that
recites that sentence and then produces a payload carrying a sealed-Run-shaped field contradicts its
own claim — the "claims contradicted by bytes" defect.

On (c), the DR-133 NT-6 name collision (G21) is a live trap; §1.5 exists so the corpus can recite the
distinction rather than trip it. `g21-fixture-corpus.v3.review-independent.codex.json` returned
`REJECT` with `G21FXV3-M1 — The non-object-top-level payload is an RF-2 case but is not authorized as
a member of the closed CC-5 corpus`, and `g21-fixture-corpus.v4.review-independent.codex.json`
returned `REJECT` with `G21FXV4-M1 — The remainder inventory still classifies non-object top level as
CC-5 and therefore does not fully land G21FXV3-M1` — two consecutive rejections on a single
misclassification and its incomplete repair. Misclassifying a G27 witness under G19 or under DR-133
NT-6 is the same defect.

On (d), `g23-fixture-corpus.v1`'s `S4 — leftoverDesignClosedIfAcceptedAndRecorded over-claims closure
of OBL-G23-FX-AUTHORING` is the template, and G27 is tempting for the same reason as G26: one class,
three members, three states, 1:1, all authored in one act. The safe form remains
`g23-fixture-corpus.v3.json#$.leftoverDesignClosedIfAcceptedAndRecorded` = `[]`.
