### Risk

The four recorded reviewer-attack patterns map onto G15 sharply. (a) *Inventing a vocabulary the
record does not carry* — the g23.v1 findings M2 ("coverageCommitment is a SHA-256 over compact JSON,
not deterministic-CBOR, and its construction is undeclared") and M3
("hostRequestedDomainBaseline invents a Coverage-domain encoding vocabulary") are exactly the shape
of an unforced error here, where a corpus must declare how each archive payload's digest is
constructed and must not name an encoding v14 has not named. (b) *Collapsing two classes the
occupancy keeps separate* — the g23.v2 finding M1 (EV-2 collapsed into EV-3) has an obvious G15
analogue in the four AT half-pairs: `AT-1-ASSEMBLY` versus `AT-1-TRUSTED-INSTALL`, `AT-4` versus
`AT-4-NEGOTIATION`, `AT-5-ASSEMBLY` versus `AT-5-TRUSTED-INSTALL`, and the twelve-report unit versus a
per-platform unit, all of which the occupancy expressly forbids collapsing (`$.reportUnit.not`,
`$.failsIf[9]`, `$.failsIf[19]`, `$.failsIf[20]`). (c) *Classifying a witness under a closed
identifier it does not belong to* — the g21.v3/v4 finding `G21FXV3-M1` maps onto the 80-key closed
map: a payload filed under an AT-ARCHIVE key whose expected fate it does not produce fails
`$.at8.passProperty`. (d) *Over-claiming closure* — the g23.v1 finding S4 maps onto
`leftoverDesignClosedIfAcceptedAndRecorded`: because `OBL-AT-FX-AUTHORING` is measured true on two
joins, a G15 corpus that lists it as closed is over-claiming on its face. The additional G15-specific
risk is that `AT-ARCHIVE-IDENTITY` cannot be produced at all without the reserved adapter, so a
corpus that produces "identity" bytes by hand is inventing the adapter's output and will be rejected
on pattern (a).

**OQ-G15-1:** Do AT fixture *bytes* depend on the adapter, or only AT *execution*? The bytes that
make this ambiguous: `packaging-leftover-join.v4.json` `$.obligations[4].reason` ("The 51 D-106
fixtures are FG-3 hand-authored DR-103 admission-class evidence, not adapter-run AT fixtures.") and
`$.obligations[2].reason` (OD-P1 "owner after condition 5") on one side;
`harness.DR-G15.packaging-adapter-conformance.v9.json` `$.at8.identityCase` ("two adapter RUNS") and

`DECISION-PACKETS/D1-fixture-authoring-delegation.md` §7 item 4 (line 284) records the same question

**OQ-G15-3:** Does any AT member count as an "oversized-input fixture" for the purposes of the adopted
C7 clause ("record measured manifest-byte / tree-entry / path-length / alias-count caps before
