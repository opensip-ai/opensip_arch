vocabulary or encoding the record does not carry, (b) collapsing two classes the occupancy keeps
separate, (c) classifying a witness under a closed identifier it does not belong to, (d) over-claiming
closure. **G28's dominant exposure is (c), and it has a second, structural exposure the other four
gates do not have.**

On (c): G28 is the gate where a closed identifier set is both present and forbidden to copy. Any
token a member cites must be a real member of `d9-exit-contract.v1.14.json`'s closed vocabulary and
must be cited by reference. `g21-fixture-corpus.v3.review-independent.codex.json` returned `REJECT`

closed-corpus misclassification and its incomplete repair. A G28 witness citing a token that is not
in `$.codeVocabulary`, or attributing a live-cell member to NT-7, lands the same finding.

The structural exposure is EV-4. The occupancy makes it simultaneously mandatory (it has an
