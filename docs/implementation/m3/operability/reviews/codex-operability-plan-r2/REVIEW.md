## Lead judgments

**O9: defer raw provider stderr capture beyond M3.** The no-text M3 path is simpler to enforce and consistent with private-source support. Counts, closed provider reason codes and carefully classified fingerprints can aid diagnosis without creating a raw-output archive. Revisit only through S-OP-9 and owner sign-off if measured support needs justify it. Any later capture must be separately classified and consented, bounded in bytes/age/retention, protected by admitted custody, excluded from ordinary logs/ring/default bundle/OTLP, and advertised as private untrusted text with honest redaction limits. A renamed SafeField or automatic bundle inclusion must not create consent. This recommendation is compatible with leaving owner approval and future capture law open.

**Second signal during commit: preserve durability, correct the bound and projection.** Commit work counters do not bound native elapsed time, and attempt admission does not mean invocation settlement. Preserve the FinalGate latch and X3D/X7 outcome joins; correct OP-R2-02/03 before accepting the plan. ### OP-R2-05 (P2) — The retention protocol does not establish its stated worst-case overshoot

Location: §3.3 retention and §10 bounds control, [PLAN.md:180](/Users/sb/code/opensip-ai/opensip_arch/docs/implementation/m3/operability/PLAN.md:180); also [§10 bounds:383](/Users/sb/code/opensip-ai/opensip_arch/docs/implementation/m3/operability/PLAN.md:383).

The 512 MiB target is described as bounded best effort with overshoot limited to concurrent writers × 64 MiB plus crash files. But a skipped or failed pruning pass does not stop rotation or creation of another 64 MiB file. A single writer can retain N closed files across repeated busy-lock passes, or when deletion fails; its accumulated excess is not limited to one active file. Active-file locking prevents deletion of live files but does not bound this backlog. The parallel-writer control therefore promises a bound the specified protocol cannot enforce.

**Required plan change:** Either give an enforceable finite allowance for unpruned growth and stop/drop persistent logging when that allowance cannot be reclaimed, including lock-busy, deletion failure and abandoned-pruner cases, or explicitly withdraw the finite overshoot guarantee and describe the aggregate as an unbounded best-effort retention target. Keep each file's real hard cap and optional-loss outcome preservation. Align the response table, O2 and §10 control with the chosen claim. A shared reservation ledger is one option, not a requirement of this review.
