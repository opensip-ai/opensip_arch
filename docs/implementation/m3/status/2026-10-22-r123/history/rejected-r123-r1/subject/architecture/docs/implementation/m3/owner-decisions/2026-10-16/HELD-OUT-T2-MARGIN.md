# Owner decision: the held-out T2 margin (2026-10-16)

**Channel.** The lead's AskUserQuestion, answered by the owner in session on 2026-10-16. This records the owner's own answer; the lead infers nothing beyond it.

**The question, verbatim.** "The T2 margin holds for all 39 lead-readable entries under the new discovery caps (2^22 objects, 2^21 edges, 2^30 bytes). B2-b is not complete until the 10 held-out T2 entries, which I must never read, also show the 2x margin. How should their margin be established?"

**The options offered, verbatim.**

1. **Mechanical blind run (Recommended).** "Authorize a Codex-reviewed held-out mode of the census tool that I run without seeing contents. It integrity-checks each held-out store and reports only pass/fail and per-mille margins per entry: no paths, names or counts. Reviewed before and after the run. Keeps the held-outs unread and completes B2-b if they pass."
2. **Law change: lead-readable suffices.** "Amend the B2-b gate (LD-B2B-4 / M3-B item 12) so the margin over the 39 lead-readable entries completes B2-b, and record the held-out margin as a qualification-time check instead. Faster, but weaker: the caps are untested on the entries kept back for an unbiased check."
3. **Someone else runs it.** "Leave the held-out run to you or a separate party with access. B2-b stays open, and dependent units (B2-c, J1) proceed on the recorded caps as provisional until results arrive."

**The owner's answer.** Option 1, "Mechanical blind run (Recommended)".

**Consequence.** The B2-b4 plan r5 adds section 2.5 (B2-b4c) to implement it.
