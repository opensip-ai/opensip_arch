This extends the requested panel recommendation. It selects no law, initial protocol major, supported-major table, refusal detail, manifest field or product behavior. Owner direction on the new uniform signed field is pending. Existing accepted interfaces remain closed.

That is a proposed new selection, never a derivation from release numbers, Rust3/TS2, analyzer protocol or another resource's declaration. An alternative is to publish the signed field/projection while withholding new resource-role admission until each interface and supported major is selected.

A compatible artifact release may preserve an interface major while changing its authenticated manifest/tree bytes and therefore its closure identity. A major declaration is not an artifact digest, compiler ABI compatibility certificate, resource admission or launch capability. Matching majors across different roles do not establish cross-role compatibility. Existing role-specific compiler identity/resource joins remain necessary; no compiler/LLVM matching rule or artifact-member list is invented here. The proposed major-bump rule and support table still need exact successor authority.

Routing must be published rather than inferred.
