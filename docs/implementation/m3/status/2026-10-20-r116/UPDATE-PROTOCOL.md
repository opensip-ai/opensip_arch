# Mandatory status-update step

The lead maintains this companion board alongside the accepted plan. The plan and amendments govern scope/order/gates; this board reports execution evidence and does not amend them.

At task selection, review submission, findings, acceptance, integration/binding, blocker changes and decisions, update the local durable checkpoint immediately. Prepare the shared board/register delta for review by the owner-designated independent reviewer and publication, bundled with the next suitable unit or as a status-only unit. A private checkpoint does not substitute for the shared board. The board always states its evidence cut-off and pending publication; it does not claim a newer state than its pins support.

After every accepted integration, reconciliation of plan status is part of completion before selecting the next unit: record exact scoped outcome, commit references, review/manifest, checks, remaining parent work, decision/blocker impact and newly ready actions. The accepted-unit implementation bytes remain closed; status review verifies recorded facts and does not re-review their code. Preserve historical board revisions and link the current revision from IMPLEMENTATION-STATUS.md. Root commit references can be sourced after integration; publish reviewed factual bytes rather than silently filling hash-pinned subjects after assent.

A blocker card names its category, owner, exact missing prerequisite, affected units and next resolution action. An architecture proposal that the lead can develop belongs in the ready queue; it is not a blanket owner wait. Explicit owner choices remain owner choices. No decision or law change is inferred from an unanswered question.

Unit status distinguishes READY, IN_PROGRESS, IN_REVIEW, CHANGES_REQUIRED, ACCEPTED_UNBOUND, INTEGRATED/BOUND, PARTIAL, BLOCKED and HELD. CLOSED applies only to the exact accepted unit scope with required binding/checks complete. Evidence-only archives, libraries, prototypes and test passes are labelled by their actual scope; no percentage-complete metric substitutes for evidence or closes a parent unit. The current active unit is shown in the durable checkpoint and the next published board delta. No fabricated active product work while blocked.

Any real scope, dependency, gate or threshold change requires the existing reviewed plan/law successor process; a status update cannot authorize it. No pushes, inventory changes, owner assent, held/private/T2 exposure or product qualification follows from this protocol.

The mutable front door IMPLEMENTATION-STATUS.md points to the current reviewed revision. Historical subject pins of this path are verified against their accepted Git commits and retained byte-identical history snapshots, not against newer front doors. Versioned records and reviews remain unchanged. Operational status is never inserted into a contract/inventory candidate or design lock.
