## Scope

Implements accepted D3b plan r3 LD-D3B-2 through LD-D3B-5 and LD-D3B-12: bounded breadth-first snapshot, group SIGKILL, identity-checked escaped-target sends, paced group-enumeration confirmation and reap-last handling on macOS. The sampler uses the same bounded listing; the previous unbounded API is retired. Adds typed TreeEvidence to settlement and TreeSwept/ToolTreeSwept facts, with the ordinary tool.tree.swept registration under LD-D3B-9. The existing production-call gate for D3b ladders stays closed. D3b-2 cancellation, revocation and clocks remain separate.

The count bound includes the root: provider 4, tool 64 total pids. Queued/inspected counts include the root, its original identity or exited observation is retained, and each queued pid is counted once. Targets and by-pid outcome counts exclude the root. The snapshot buffers do not grow beyond the caller's remaining bound. Late joiners are deduplicated to the walk bound with explicit saturation. Killed is only distinct identity-checked Sent outcomes, never group delivery or inferred deaths. Group kills are attempts, with successes derivable as attempts minus failures. Every original failed native LaunchError is retained with the step in call order, including its call name and optional errno; no absent errno becomes zero. Evidence is borrowed from settlement because original errors are owned and not Copy. No descendant pid is added to an operational record.

An exceeded confirmation remains incomplete even after a later singleton. Native failures, changed identity and refused sends also prevent a completed report. A positive exit observation plus a singleton, complete snapshot and no incomplete observations permits only GroupKilledPlatformLimited. Failed confirmation listings latch and end confirmation; an exceeded listing repeats at the accepted cadence until singleton or ceiling while retaining its incomplete observation. The ordinary loop serves channels and triggers between confirmations. No group call follows reap or abandonment.
