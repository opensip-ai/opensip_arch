# Funding calculation versus the callback accounting rule

This is a conditional source/model compatibility argument, not a demonstrated native undercharge, an adoption of one global M model, a fee correction or full source-bound qualification. The accepted callback rules explicitly govern their callback sub-proof only. The old fixed128/scan12F allowances were not established under those rules. No published/product/draft/queued source, coefficient, cap or authority is changed here.

Hypothesis: extend that exact nonleaf argument/nonunit-return transport rule to funding, retain its existing finite semantic leaf set, and do not newly declare caller-defined add/mul or closure-dispatching Option::ok_or_else as semantic leaves. A successful add/mul helper has at least five visits: its nonempty argument transport, one checked scalar arithmetic primitive, its nonempty return transport, and Option::ok_or_else's nonempty receiver transport and nonunit Result return transport. Count neither ok_or_else body/tag construction/match nor ? propagation nor work ledger/iteration/getters/casts/remaining operations. Every listed nonempty tuple carries an actual usize on the successful path and therefore has positive active width; ceil(2w/64)>=1. This lower bound requires no native enum padding/layout estimate and makes no actual compiler/machine-copy claim. Any wider tuple or finer body accounting only raises it.

The helpers' source bodies are checked_add/checked_mul followed by ok_or_else(arithmetic). The captured Option function has a match with an err:FnOnce() callback in the None branch, so it is not within the callback rule's direct field/tag query or fixed usize/bool primitive list. The success branch's receiver/result each carry usize; the error callback is not executed in this successful-path lower bound. The caller-defined helper itself is explicitly outside that rule's leaf whitelist. A future alternative model may propose a precisely bounded checked-arithmetic/carrier primitive, but that is an additional substantive choice and cannot silently be inferred just to make the old constants fit.

For a nonempty candidate whose funding arithmetic succeeds, the exact source contains32 add/mul calls outside the scan loop, including the two in the count>0 index branch. The Blob scan body contains five calls per Blob. Thus these calls alone require at least160 fixed visits and25F scan visits under the hypothesis. The stated fixed128 and scan12F blocks cannot individually fund those lower bounds. In particular at F=1 this successful-path helper-only lower bound is185 visits whereas the total preprocessing prepayment is128+12=140. Both the scan and the rest of pricing execute before the final remainder charge. This does not charge actual M a second time or claim the whole M fee is185: it compares only the work required to compute that fee with the earlier prepayment under the hypothetical extension.

The F=1 numeric valuation used by the pure checker has B=0,P=9,E=2 and small representable formula results, so the arithmetic contradiction is not caused by overflow. It is not a physical fixture or a proof that an actual admitted role/candidate/run with that valuation was constructed. More generally the32-call statement is conditional on any lawful nonempty candidate reaching successful arithmetic; no hash/signature/custody/admission is inferred. A refusal prefix may execute fewer calls; this argument does not fabricate a native failure or assert every refusal consumes this bound.
