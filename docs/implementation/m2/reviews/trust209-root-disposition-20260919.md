Ordinary guarded PRESENT can heal quorum loss; restored quorum alone cannot. CLOCK never preempts recovery. Revocation cannot be laundered through a reset followed by ordinary PRESENT.
- Source trust standing and original transition attribution change in one authoritative capsule revision outside all evidence store roots. Attribution survives ordinary payloads and active-slot retirement. Whole-install/coordinated trust rollback remains outside local detection.
- Root ceremony and clock-floor recovery are separate. A begun ceremony binds its payload identity; a different payload requires an explicit termination/restart. Accepted exits must define disposition of staged/begun data in the same revision. New changed-store Phase C refuses an active source or ancestor-target ceremony before effects. Historical Phase B performs no fresh trust evaluation.
- Prepared trust continuity is separate from the unchanged six-counter floor image. Exact source-before/source-after and target-before/target-after identities avoid both a false source digest mismatch after fencing and the invalid comparison of source-only history with merged target history. After-images and their immutable audit dependencies precede fencing; publication remains ordered single-file steps, not a cross-file transaction.

The target's retained root chain remains its anchor until an admitted chain meets the carried floor. All eleven clock fields still require admission; projected six counters alone are insufficient.
- Immutable audit and revocation dependencies are retained in the first implementation; no automatic GC or loss of accepted observations through recovery. Reference addresses include identity sufficient for direct lookup across store transitions. Directory listing never establishes a selected accepted root or committed event.

## Corrections still required before adopting the proposal

1. **Genesis and rejected attempts.** Positive clock counters rule out zero-filled genesis. However, null-clock/all-initial equivalence alone does not settle write-ahead evaluation on a validly signed but rejected initial payload, attempted recovery before first TRUSTED entry, or per-role acceptance when a global root already exists. An exact creator/first-payload law must reconcile S4's write-ahead floor, the complete eleven-member decoder and v14's per-role meaning. Do not create synthetic accepted documents or use an audit reason as proof of history.
2. **Revocation history bounds.** A binary merge DAG's depth does not bound its node count or total bytes. The proposed reader needs independent limits on unique nodes, read bytes, memory, cycle/duplicate handling and metadata list bounds, with exhaustion always unavailable. An append can durably retain a newly authenticated observation even if later evaluation exhausts, but “always accept a valid newer list” does not bypass authentication: verifying signer revocation itself may require the prior complete history. Admission failure cannot publish guessed authority. No node-budget number or compaction protocol is selected yet.
3. **Evidence producers already partly exist.** Claude's note says signature/threshold producers are not implemented in a tree it has.211 does contain security/trust.rs private ValidatedRootPayload, SignatureEvidence, QuorumReport, verified_envelopes, RevocationEvidence and observe_revocation. They retain root/message/role/namespace/signer/threshold/document evidence. Current revocation population, actor/time/custody and durable joins remain conditional. Reuse those owners rather than introduce duplicate verification. EV-QUORUM's available-key cardinality (v1 §4.1) must also be distinguished from a presented document's signature threshold; the two are not interchangeable.
4. **Root-recovery authorization.** The current clock-floor TrustRecoveryEpochV1 is not a root-ceremony application. v14 requires an old-root/new-root/role binding and positive authorization distinct from replacement self-signature. A concrete schema, preimage/domain, threshold producer and import/staging owner must be located or explicitly designed and reviewed; metadata payload hashes alone do not supply that authorization. Do not silently repurpose an existing signed kind.
5. **Audit limits and ordering.** The 4KiB estimate is withdrawn. Preflight encoded records before effects under a specified bound; preserve the existing event and separate abort termination semantics. Distinguish a completed refused attempt, an unpublished orphan and failure to durably record an attempt. “Every attempted event” cannot imply a guaranteed write after process death or storage failure. Evidence records require independently checked causal joins, not just sequence numbers.
6. **Restore declaration.** No current carrier/writer/reader for the historical “restore marker” has been found. Whole-store restore and portable evidence adoption differ. No new restore CLI or implicit restoration has been selected. A current owner must specify how declared restored bytes remain unusable until a lawful reset/import, how a present active transition or ceremony blocks that path, and how previous fence attribution remains interpretable. Directory census may detect inconsistency; it must not auto-accept roots or merge counters as repair.
7. **Snapshot pins.** Incorporate exact v14/v2 clauses plus current supersessions into the reference successor; do not adopt the entire historical candidate indiscriminately.208/211 product owner-inputs retain their exact bytes, but201 reference itself has not been superseded.
