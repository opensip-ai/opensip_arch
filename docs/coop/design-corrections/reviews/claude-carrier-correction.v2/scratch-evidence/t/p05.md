
No physical DDL anywhere in Source25 admits SEAL: all four carriers of the DDL text (v1
markdown, v2 .sql, security_unit_lib_v2, security_unit_lib_v8) share the identical 14-member
set. So the current logical journal cannot be written to any inherited physical carrier at all,
and TERMINAL, which the physical carrier REQUIRES at the reserved slot, is not a member of the
public schema-3 JournalRecord. The two facts are jointly unsatisfiable, which is why loosening
an enum is not an option: it would either admit TERMINAL as a public schema-3 record or leave
SEAL unwritable.
