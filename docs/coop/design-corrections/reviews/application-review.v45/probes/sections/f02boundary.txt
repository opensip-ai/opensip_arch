273: ## Current V1 product boundary and required successors
274: 
275: V1 product authority currently says:
276: 
277: - P-1: no marketplace, public lifecycle parity, discovery service, or ecosystem
278:   governance depth;
279: - P-2: contributions are narrow producers or data-only rules/profiles, with no
280:   root commands, policy, persistence, rendering, termination, or host lifecycle;
281: - G3: full-default Rust host/core plus bundled TypeScript and pinned Rust
282:   sidecar/offline assets is the V1 substrate.
283: 
284: An independently released public catalog/lifecycle may exceed all three. Before
285: acceptance, product owner DR-117 must explicitly disposition:
286: 
287: 1. marketplace/catalog and governance depth;
288: 2. external lifecycle parity and discovery;
289: 3. contribution roles beyond narrow/data-only;
290: 4. untrusted native or WASM admission and required enforcement evidence;
291: 5. imperative contributions, probes, project hooks, and root commands;
292: 6. network-granted analysis and egress defaults; and
293: 7. replacement of the full-default G3 physical substrate.
294: 
295: Until then, V1 exclusions remain: no ecosystem-depth promise, untrusted
296: native/WASM, imperative contributions, probes, or network-granted analysis by
297: default. V2 may define a future-safe boundary but cannot ship those policies by
298: architecture prose.
