## Decision

1. Adopt `docs/v2/architecture/13-evidence-workflows-and-product-contracts.md`
   as the product's design supplement, with its distinction between existing
   preview guidance and future capability constraints. Its ownership and
   evidence requirements govern subsequent design of those capabilities;
   they are not merely a list of suggestions for reconsideration after coding.
2. Retain `docs/coop/FALLOW-BORROW-REGISTER.md` as a source/gap map over all
   seven original and eight additional ideas. `PRESERVE` means no missing
   foundation is asserted, `ADAPT` identifies the design consequence, and
   `FUTURE` defers implementation while retaining the adopted constraints.
   Algorithm selection, exact future schemas, and support dates are not
   determined by a competitor's feature list.
3. Preserve the host authority boundary, C-1 sufficiency, existing fingerprint
   and detector-pivot recipes, and data-only workflows. Model-based similarity
   and external judgment remain advisory; no best-effort semantic substitution,
   producer verdict authority, implicit effects, or global confidence ladder
   is borrowed.
4. Link the supplement through `docs/START-HERE.md`,
   `docs/v2/architecture/README.md`, and the design-decisions section of
   `docs/v2/architecture/10-mvp-and-future-scope.md`. Preserve the exact preview
   handoff, application manifest, file-08 after-image, V1 chapters, and their
   frozen evidence. The three navigation edits and two design documents are
   reviewed together with this act.
5. Adopt no new preview command, schema field, identifier recipe, predicate,
   state class, corpus, numeric threshold, platform, gate, or runtime effect.
   The supplement's new capability requirements apply at their later scope
   re-entry under the existing owning rows. It neither authorizes product
   implementation nor claims that future capabilities are implementation-ready.

## Alternatives and rationale

- Defer all consideration until implementation: rejected because repair,
  review, and evidence handoffs could otherwise require retrofitting ownership
  and public contracts.
- Expand the preview to implement Fallow's feature roster: rejected because
  the design lessons do not require that product/schedule decision.
- Rewrite frozen architecture chapters and repin the completion application:
  unnecessary for an additive design supplement that changes no accepted
  preview behavior. A future semantic change still requires exact successors.
- Keep only a non-authoritative borrow checklist: insufficient for the user's
  request to update the design now. Chapter 13 carries the adopted constraints;
  the borrow register only supplies traceability.

The design-selection decision is preference-laden and reversible. The
preservation of existing semantic and authority laws is rule-governed.
